My Journey

My love for sports began when I was six years old, when I first picked up a soccer ball. I immediately fell in love with the game and worked hard to improve, eventually earning a spot on my school team at the age of nine. As the starting left winger, I loved using my speed to get down the field, create opportunities, and score goals. When my family moved from Nigeria to Canada, everything changed. Leaving behind my friends, my familiar environment, and even my soccer cleats was difficult. At first, I was disappointed, but that disappointment led me to discover something that would change my life forever: basketball. I picked up a basketball for the first time shortly after arriving in Canada. The game felt completely different from soccer, but I was determined to learn. I spent countless hours at local courts practicing, competing, and developing my skills. What started as a way to stay active quickly became a passion. I fell in love with the fast pace, the teamwork, and the challenge of constantly improving. Since then, basketball has become more than just a sport to me. It has taught me discipline, resilience, and the value of hard work. Every workout, practice, and game motivates me to become a better player and person. Today, I am fully committed to pursuing my goals in basketball and continuing to push myself to reach the highest level possible.

Future Goals

My ultimate goal is to play college basketball at a high level and eventually make it to the professional leagues. I am dedicated to putting in the work necessary to achieve this dream, including rigorous training, competing in tournaments, and seeking out opportunities to showcase my skills. I am also focused on developing as an elite 2-Way Guard, becoming recognized as one of the best defenders in the country while scoring points at a high efficiency. I am committed to continuous improvement and am excited about the journey ahead as I work towards reaching my full potential in basketball.
