About Me

My name is Dipo, and I am a dedicated basketball player from Winnipeg, Manitoba. Basketball is my passion, and I work every day to improve my skills, leadership, and understanding of the game.

My Basketball Journey

I first fell in love with basketball when I was 9 years old. I got invited to play a basketball game when I was new to the school. I didn't know anyone, so it felt nice to be invited. Back then, I couldn't play very well, but my teamates still encouraged me. Ever since then, I've always loved the sports and commited to getting better day by day. What started as a love for the game has grown into a serious pursuit of collegiate and professional basketball. Through training, games, tournaments, and recruiting efforts, I continue to develop as both a player and a person.
