Playing Style

My game is inspired by Dwyane Wade and Jimmy Butler. I enjoy attacking the basket, finishing through contact, and impacting the game on both ends of the floor. I take pride in my competitiveness, defensive effort, and ability to help my team win.

Around the basket, I often finish off 2 feet, utilizing either a Hop Step or a Pro Hop. When needed, I'll use a Euro step or a swing step if I need to get around my defender. Unless absolutely needed, I will almost never adjust my positioning in mid-air, as it makes me unbalanced.

On defense, I am a tenacious pressure defender who applies pressure to the Ball Handler, making them uncomfortable. I take pride in my ability to stay in front of my man and make it difficult for them to get to the basket. I also take pride in my ability to read the offense and make smart decisions on when to help and when to stay with my assignment.
